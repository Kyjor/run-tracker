Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Run 4 Fun (“the App”) is a running and training companion. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Account & sync

If you create an account, we store your email, display name, and synced training data (runs, plans, goals, gear, and related social content) using Supabase so you can sign in across devices and use friends / community features.

On-device data

Run history, settings, plans, and related data also live in a local database on your device. You can export a backup from Settings.

Health, location & sensors

  • Apple Health (HealthKit) — with your permission, the App can read workouts and related metrics to import runs, and may write runs you choose to export.
  • Location — used during live GPS runs to record your route. Background location is only used while a live run is active.
  • Bluetooth — optional connection to heart-rate monitors during live runs.

Advertising

The App may show ads (Google AdMob) on the home screen. You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive purchase status needed to unlock “Remove Ads” on your device; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Maps

Route maps may load map tiles from third-party map providers. Those providers may receive approximate network requests needed to render the map.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
